Platerizer

Hash-only car hazard alerts

Platerizer warns drivers about cars that other Platerizer users have flagged nearby. Tap a car on your dash-mounted phone's live camera view; the app reads that car's license plate on the phone itself, turns it into a one-way hash, and shares only that hash — never the plate — with other Platerizer users. When two or more different people flag the same car, everyone else driving with Platerizer gets a spoken hazard alert, with no plate number ever shown or searchable.
