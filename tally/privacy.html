Summary

Tally stores your data on your device only. We do not collect it, we cannot see it, and we have no way to receive it — the app contains no networking code of any kind.

What we collect

Nothing. Tally has no analytics, no crash reporter, no account system and no network access, so there is no data type for Hearthworks (the developer) to collect. The table below exists to show exactly what data the app creates and stores, all of it on your device only:

data why where it is stored who can see it retention
Counter name (text you type) to label the thing you're counting on-device only, in the app's local SwiftData/SQLite store only you, on this device until you swipe-delete the counter or delete the app
Counter creation date sorts your counters, oldest first on-device only, same store only you same as above
Tap timestamp builds the per-counter history the app exists to show you on-device only, same store only you until you long-press to remove the latest tap, delete the counter, or delete the app

This table summarizes the user-facing items from apps/tally/factory/design.md's privacy data inventory, which additionally lists purely internal-framework items (the counter↔event relationship, SwiftData's internal PersistentIdentifier, test-only store copies created solely by an XCUITest launch argument, and framework-written UserDefaults housekeeping) — none of them user-typed, none of them different in kind from the rows above: on-device only, never linked to identity, never used for tracking. privacy-labels.json's privacy_manifest_consistency block and PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy's CA92.1 entry account for the UserDefaults item specifically. No item in either the table above or the fuller design.md inventory ever leaves the device.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics SDK of any kind (confirmed: no Firebase, no Sentry, no AppsFlyer, no similar symbol anywhere in the app's source).
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA), no ATTrackingManager, no AdSupport framework.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no user profiles, no email address, no name beyond the counter labels you choose to type (which describe what you're counting, not you).
  • No location, camera, photo library, microphone, contacts, health, or calendar data — Tally never requests any of these permissions.
  • No network requests of any kind — there is no URLSession, no web view, and no code path in the app that can send or receive data over a network.

iCloud / CloudKit

Not applicable. Tally does not use iCloud or CloudKit (cloudkit: false in the app's build configuration). There is no sync, no shared container, and no iCloud account state the app reads. Your data lives on one device and does not travel with you to a second device.

Purchases

Not applicable. Tally has no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no StoreKit products in this version.

Third parties

None. Tally has zero third-party dependencies (no Swift packages, no binary frameworks — see apps/tally/project.yml, which has no packages: section) and shares data with no third party, because it collects none to share.

Children

Tally has no user accounts, no chat or messaging, no user-generated content shared with anyone else, and no advertising. Nothing about the app depends on knowing a user's age, and it collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It is rated 4+ (see metadata.md).

Your rights

Because everything Tally stores lives only on your device:

  • Delete a single counter and its history: swipe left on the counter in the list and tap Delete. This removes it immediately (no confirmation, no undo — standard for this destructive action).
  • Delete everything: delete the app. Its local store is removed with it.
  • Export: not available in this version; there is nothing to request from Hearthworks because Hearthworks never receives your data in the first place.
  • Questions: contact us at the email above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be dated at the top and made available at the hosting URL on the app's App Store Connect record. There is no mailing list or in-app notice mechanism in this version, since the app has no network access to deliver one.
