Support — Tally

How to get help

Email mark@hearthworks.io. That address reaches a person, not a ticket queue.

Useful things to include: your iOS version, your device, what you did, and what happened instead. Screenshots help. Hearthworks is a small operation, so expect a human reply rather than an instant one.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Tally has no accounts, no sign-in and no password. Nothing you enter is tied to an identity.

Where is my data stored?

On your device, in the app's own storage. It is not uploaded anywhere, so there is no copy of it on anyone else's computer.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual items inside the app, or delete the app itself — everything it stored goes with it. There is no server copy to ask anyone to erase.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Tally does not need a network connection.

Something is broken, or I want a feature.

Email mark@hearthworks.io and say what you did and what happened instead. Bug reports with the steps that cause the problem get fixed first.

Privacy

What Tally stores, and what it does not, is written out in the privacy policy.
